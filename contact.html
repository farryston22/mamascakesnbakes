Good to know

A few answers before you get in touch.

How far ahead should I order?

As early as you can — especially for weekends, tiered cakes and busy seasons. Message us to check your date.

Can you copy a design I've seen?

Share your inspiration photo and we'll create our own take on it, tailored to your theme and colours.

Do you make cupcakes and treats?

Yes — cupcake boxes, number cakes and dessert platters, on their own or to match your cake.

Dietary requirements?

Please mention any allergies or dietary needs in your enquiry so we can discuss what's possible.
